# AI FinOps policy
Default READ ONLY / PLAN ONLY. No Lovable generation, remix, creation, paid retry or bulk execution without explicit per-application authorization. GitHub-first feature branches; no production edits without approval. Record baseline balance, approved scope, estimated exposure (or unknown), actual balance and evidence after each authorized build. One paid build at a time. Proposed reserve 30%; halt before reserve is threatened. Estimates are not hard caps; if no enforceable cap exists, obtain explicit acceptance of exposure before execution. Never retry automatically. Escalate unexpected consumption; stop additional requests and reconcile billing history. No monitoring job may invoke AI generation.
